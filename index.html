I'm a Mechanical Engineering senior (Aerospace Engineering minor) seeking a full-time engineering role after I graduate in May 2027. I'm particularly interested in fluid dynamics, heat transfer and mechanical design.

I'm currently working on two independent projects. The first is an Ansys Fluent free-surface CFD study to pinpoint where a 19-ft sailboat hull transitions from displacement to planing, with results to be published in the International Lightning Class Association newsletter. The second is an air engine I'm designing and building: modeling it in Siemens NX, analyzing its kinematics, loads and vibration with NX Motion Simulation and Nastran FEM, and fabricating its parts through CNC and manual machining, composite layup, casting, sheet metal and injection molding.

I'd especially like to work in the marine/maritime tech, aerospace, renewable energy, defense, or advanced transportation sectors.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
